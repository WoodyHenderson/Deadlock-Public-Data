## Patch-note-only behavior

The official note additionally says:

- Threadsap's enemy Spirit reduction ends when Baba dies.
- Repeated airborne Granny Long Legs light melees have diminishing forward momentum; the upward launch is reduced; and heavy-melee ground pound no longer gains horizontal speed when landing on slopes.
- Danny's charged shots change from 44 to 48, Chimera Mk1 airborne slow effectiveness is reduced by 15%, and Rocket Jumping self-damage changes from 6.5% to 5% max health and becomes Spirit damage.

The reviewed client-6785 hero/weapon/ability records do not directly expose these behaviors or values. In particular, Danny's note statements are not inferred from base `BulletDamage`, `MaxHealthSelfDamagePercent`, or removal of the heat-gain field. Treat them as official patch-note intent, not independent runtime observations.
