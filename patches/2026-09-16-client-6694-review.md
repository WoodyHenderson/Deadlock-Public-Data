# September 16 client 6694 scoped data refresh

This refresh advances the dated records listed here; it does **not** replace
the repository-wide September 3 baseline. It compares client 6684 / source
revision 10933105 with client 6694 / source revision 11005995 at immutable
`deadlock-data` commit
[`fc4f540f12e019a6a2a422e0818917a4eedaf881`](https://github.com/deadlock-wiki/deadlock-data/tree/fc4f540f12e019a6a2a422e0818917a4eedaf881).
The exact file and API response hashes are recorded in the
[source registry](../sources/source-registry.yaml); the raw September 16 note is
[archived separately](raw/2026-09-16.txt).

## Records advanced

- All **38 selectable hero** YAML/Markdown records now use client-6694 hero,
  ability, card, and English-localization data. The hero, ability, and card
  datasets differ from client 6684 in 10 hero-data keys, 103 ability-data keys,
  and 82 hero ability-card slots. These generated-field differences are not
  independent runtime tests.
- All **173 purchasable item** records now use the client-6694 versioned API
  response and matching secondary datasets. Forty primary item records differ
  from client 6684, including the 22 named in the notes and additional
  slow-related item cards. Component relationships, displayed fields, and
  descriptions are source-pinned to this same dated snapshot.
- The hero and item rosters state their client-6694 pins. Specific general
  economy, respawn, breakable-timing, slow, and objective records below are
  advanced separately; unrelated records retain their earlier provenance.

## Patch-note-only behavior and unresolved formulas

The notes describe parry pausing reload, stun pausing rather than restarting
reload, a regular-dash parry canceling the dash and momentum, and dashes/light
melee no longer pausing weapon-cycle time. These are labeled patch-note evidence,
not client-field proof or independent runtime tests. Weapon-cycle time and reload
timing are kept distinct.

The notes also report removal of fixed extra comeback bounties and a general
reduction. The retained hero-kill multiplier fields do not supply the replacement
runtime curve. Golden Goose Egg stored Souls counting toward net worth and
Plated Armor on-hit Spirit-proc interactions remain patch-note-only because the
structured item records do not expose those behaviors. Exact hero-kill and Rift
comeback formulas remain unresolved.

Silver's article and the September 17 Melee Attack revision are later, separately
pinned cross-checks; they are not backdated as September 16 runtime proof. The
September 9 chat/ping limits belong to the earlier update, not this patch.

## Snapshot boundary

This is a domain-scoped September 16 snapshot. It does not advance unrelated
records, infer behavior from field names, or assert that client configuration
proves live availability. Later updates—including October 2 Rat King release
and subsequent balance changes—belong to separate dated records.
