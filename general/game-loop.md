# Core Match Loop

Deadlock is played by two opposing teams on The Cursed Apple. The match is
organized around three lanes. Each team advances its Troopers down those lanes,
earns Souls, becomes stronger, destroys enemy structures, and ultimately destroys
the enemy Patron.

## Match Start

The pre-game phase lasts 30 seconds. Players can request lane swaps during this
phase. Each player begins with 600 Souls, which is also enough for the first
ability unlock.

The three lanes are:

| Lane | UI color | Alternate name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| York | Yellow | Yellow Lane |
| Broadway | Blue | Blue Lane |
| Park | Green | Greenwich, Green Lane |

## Lane Play

Trooper waves travel into every lane and fight opposing Troopers, heroes, and
structures. Killing enemy Troopers is the most reliable early source of Souls.
Players can secure their own floating Soul Orbs or deny enemy floating orbs,
making control of each wave both an economy and territory contest.

Advancing allied Troopers pushes the team's lane territory forward. Zipline
availability follows that lane progress, so a pushed lane also improves access
to the forward part of the map.

The early period in which players primarily contest Trooper waves in assigned
lanes is commonly called the laning phase. The wiki's catch-up rule treats eight
minutes as the end of the laning phase for that mechanic, but this is not a hard
phase transition for all gameplay systems.

## Progression and Spending

Souls serve as both currency and experience. Gathering them grants Boons,
ability unlocks, and ability points at fixed thresholds. Souls can also be spent
at Curiosity Shops. Spending does not reduce the total gathered-Souls progress
used for levels.

Additional Souls and buffs are available away from the lanes through neutral
camps, breakables, Sinner's Sacrifice machines, timed powerups, the Soul Urn,
and other map events. Leaving a lane to collect these resources trades lane
presence for another form of progression.

## Objective Progression

Each lane leads through the following enemy defenses:

1. Lane Guardian
2. Walker
3. Pair of Base Guardians

The enemy base contains two Shrines and the Patron. Both Shrines must be
destroyed to expose the Patron. The Patron has two phases; defeating its second,
weakened phase wins the match.

Destroying Walkers also unlocks the team's three Extra Slots, one per enemy
Walker. Destroying one Shrine upgrades allied Troopers in its corresponding side
lane into Super Troopers; destroying both Shrines also upgrades the Blue lane.
Structure bounties award Souls to the attacking team, and defeating specific
structures improves lane pressure or unlocks later objectives.

## Team Objectives

The Mid-Boss is a neutral team objective in the center of the map. Defeating it
causes a Rejuvenator crystal to appear. Heavy melee hits claim up to three team
revive credits. A credit causes a hero who dies while the team has credits to
revive at the death location after a short delay, consuming a credit.

The Soul Urn, powerups, and Unstable Rifts create recurring reasons to contest
areas outside the lanes. These events can award Souls, temporary power, or
additional lane pressure.

## Victory Condition

There is no score-based victory. A team wins only by progressing into the enemy
base, exposing the Patron by destroying both Shrines, and destroying the Patron
through both of its phases.
