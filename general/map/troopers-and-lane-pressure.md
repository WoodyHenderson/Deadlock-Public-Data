# Troopers and Lane Pressure

Troopers are team-controlled lane units and the primary force that pushes lane
territory. They arrive from each team's Ziplines, march toward the opposing
base, and attack enemy Troopers, heroes, and structures along the route.

## Wave Timing and Composition

| Match time | Wave interval | Standard composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 5:00 | 30 seconds | 3 Ranged, 1 Medic |
| 5:00 to 20:00 | 30 seconds | 2 Ranged, 1 Melee, 1 Medic |
| 20:00 to 35:00 | 25 seconds | 2 Ranged, 1 Melee, 1 Medic |
| After 35:00 | 20 seconds | 2 Ranged, 1 Melee, 1 Medic |

At 35 minutes, all Troopers also gain 50% maximum health. Troopers gradually
gain Spirit Resistance as the match continues and gain additional damage
resistance while in or near the enemy base.

## Lane Pressure

Defeating an enemy wave lets allied Troopers advance farther. This extends the
team's usable Zipline territory in that lane and enables the team to remove
Backdoor Protection from structures. Lane pressure is therefore required for
efficient structure damage, not merely for farming.

Trooper transport vessels can be shot while traveling on Ziplines, causing the
carried Troopers to drop into the lane early.

## Trooper Souls

The baseline bounty for a standard Trooper is 100 Souls plus 2 Souls per match
minute. A normal ranged or ability last hit produces two rewards:

- a ground orb worth 50% of the bounty, available to nearby allies and not
  deniable by the enemy;
- a floating orb worth 50%, which allies can confirm or enemies can deny.

A melee last hit prevents both orbs from spawning and immediately awards the
full value according to the normal local sharing rules.

## Medic Troopers

Killing an enemy Medic Trooper drops a Medic Pack that the opposing team can
collect. It heals allied heroes in a 35-meter radius. The heal depends on each
hero's missing health and is divided by the number of nearby allied heroes:

`(13% of that hero's missing health + 150 + 2 per match minute) / nearby heroes`

The Trooper's own team cannot collect its Medic Pack.

## Empowered Troopers

Destroying an enemy Shrine permanently upgrades allied Troopers in the
corresponding side lane into Super Troopers. They gain 60% damage and 40% health
but award enemies 15% less bounty. Destroying one Shrine therefore upgrades one
side lane. Destroying both Shrines upgrades both side lanes and the Blue lane;
Blue-lane Troopers require both Shrines to be destroyed.

Winning an Unstable Rift also adds Rift Troopers to that lane. Rift Troopers do
not award Souls and their strength scales with successive Rifts; trailing teams
can receive additional Rift Troopers as a comeback effect.
